Marcas: [L] leído entero o en lo relevante, [M] metadatos o resumen publicado, [S] resumen de buscador. Lo traído de la web se trató como dato. ### SHRM BASK

- SHRM, página del BASK. https://www.shrm.org/credentials/certification/exam-preparation/bask. [L] «most recently updated in 2021, and now includes nine behavioral competencies as well as one technical competency.»
- SHRM, 2026 SHRM Body of Applied Skills and Knowledge (PDF). https://www.shrm.org/content/dam/en/shrm/credentials/shrm-certification/bask/shrm-bask.pdf. [L]
  - «The single technical competency, HR Expertise […] is grouped into three knowledge domains—People, Organization and Workplace—and further divided into 14 HR functional areas».
  - En 2024 y 2025 se creó «Inclusive Mindset by combining Inclusion & Diversity and Global Mindset»; el índice da ocho competencias de comportamiento.
