## La posición ACSM 2026: existe, cita y qué dice

**Existe y está en acceso abierto** (CC BY-NC-ND), leída completa en Europe PMC.

- Cita: Currier BS, D'Souza AC, Singh MAF, Lowisz CV, Rawson ES, Schoenfeld BJ, Smith-Ryan AE, Steen JP, Thomas GA, Triplett NT, Washington TA, Werner TJ, Phillips SM. *American College of Sports Medicine Position Stand. Resistance Training Prescription for Muscle Function, Hypertrophy, and Physical Performance in Healthy Adults: An Overview of Reviews.* Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise. 2026 abr;58(4):851-872. DOI 10.1249/MSS.0000000000003897. PMID 41843416. PMCID PMC12965823. Publicada en línea el 2026-03-05.
- URL del texto: https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000003897 y https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/
- El comunicado https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/ devolvió 403 o un desafío de Cloudflare a curl y a WebFetch. **No se pudo leer el comunicado.** Todo lo de abajo sale del artículo.

Qué dice de verdad (citas del texto completo):

- Diseño: es una revisión de revisiones con 137 revisiones sistemáticas de ensayos aleatorizados y más de 30.000 participantes. Incluye adultos sanos de 18 años o más y programas de al menos 6 semanas. Actualiza la posición de 2009. "much of the evidence synthesized here is from inexperienced trainees".
- Hallazgo central: "Few RT prescription (RTx) variables affected primary adaptations." / "Training to momentary muscle fatigue, equipment type, exercise complexity, set structure, time under tension, blood flow restriction, and periodization did not consistently impact training outcomes."
- Fuerza (Tabla 6): "Frequency: ≥2 sessions/wk; Intensity: ≥80% 1RM (dose-response); Type: Eccentric flywheel RT; Technique: Full range of motion; Volume: 2–3 sets/session; Exercise order: Beginning of training session".
- Hipertrofia (Tabla 6): "Type: Eccentric contractions/overload; Volume: ≥10 sets/wk (dose-response)"; en el texto: "higher volume (≥10 sets/muscle group/wk)".
- Hipertrofia, lo que no la cambió: "low (1 d/wk) versus high (>5 d/wk) frequency when total volume was equated; low (30% 1RM) to high (100% 1RM) loads; contractions to muscle failure/fatigue; blood flow restriction; varying loads during repetitions; fast (0.5 s) versus slow (8 s) repetitions; power training techniques; periodization (linear, undulating, and nonperiodized); or exercise order".
- Hipertrofia, datos insuficientes: máquinas frente a peso libre, monoarticular frente a multiarticular, descanso entre series, rango de movimiento, autorregulación y otras.
- Fuerza, lo que no la cambió: fallo, "machines versus free-weight RT", superficies inestables, tiempo bajo tensión, hora del día, descanso entre series ("short (<1 min) versus long (>1 min)"), tipo de contracción, entrenamiento de potencia y estructura de series. Datos insuficientes para la periodización: "one review found that periodized programs were slightly favored over nonperiodized programs to maximize strength gains under volume-equated conditions".
- Periodización: "With appropriate progressive overload, periodization is not significantly superior to nonperiodized programs; thus, periodization is less important than previously hypothesized".
- Potencia: "moderate loads (30%–70% one-repetition maximum), low-to-moderate volume (≤24 repetitions⋅sets), Olympic-style weightlifting, and power RT".
- Recomendación principal: "Our primary recommendation is that healthy adults perform RT with high effort [...] at least twice weekly, with all major muscle groups being engaged." Y: "individuals should prioritize completing any form of RT".
- Progresión: "progression is not necessary to achieve beneficial outcomes, and overload, or more accurately, increasing the stimulus in some manner, is likely a requirement only for those seeking continued longer term progress."
- Series: "healthy adults are advised to complete at least two sets per exercise". Cita un metaanálisis con "diminishing returns beyond ~2–3 sets/exercise for strength and ~18–20 weekly sets for hypertrophy".
- Fallo y RIR: "Completing sets to fatigue (momentary muscular failure) does not enhance gains in strength, hypertrophy, and power [...] 'near-failure' or a target of 2–3 repetitions in reserve (RIR)". También: "there is insufficient evidence to quantify exact RIR and perceived exertion targets".
- Bandas, casa y peso del cuerpo: "elastic band RT has been shown to increase strength, hypertrophy, and certain components of physical function. Similarly, home-based RT improved strength, muscular endurance, and balance." Sobre el peso del cuerpo: "Body weight-supported RT was a portion of some reviews, but was not studied as a stand-alone intervention except in the case of the Nordic hamstring curl." **El peso del cuerpo no se estudió por separado.** La frase "bandas, peso corporal y rutinas en casa dan beneficios notables", que la base atribuía al comunicado, no se sostiene para el peso del cuerpo.
- Seguridad: "In an analysis of >38,000 participants (>6700 RT participants) [...] exercise did not increase the risk of serious adverse events."
- Sobre la posición 2009: "both the current (11) and prior (12) Position Stands were criticized (13,14) for lacking evidence-based rigor". Sobre el consejo de 2009 de 2 a 3 sesiones de cuerpo completo para principiantes: "this recommendation was informed by only three studies [...] (n = 59 total participants)".
- Descarga o deload: el texto no la menciona.

## Menciones de la ACSM 2026 fuera de Musculación (no editadas; propuestas)

Ver la respuesta del agente. Resumen: se sostienen la idea de "pocas variables", la periodización sin efecto consistente, el 80 %, las 2-3 series y las ≥10 series, y la potencia frente a no entrenar. Hay que corregir en todos lados la frase "bandas, peso corporal y rutinas en casa", porque el peso del cuerpo no se estudió por separado. También hay que cambiar "según su resumen" o "comunicado" por "texto completo" y matizar "fuerza progresiva" con la cita sobre progresión.
